Services

Brand Identity, Copywriting

Brief

A modern, inclusive identity for a museum reimagining how visitors experience sound.

The Museum of Sound's branding centers on accessibility and immersion — a move away from the traditional, static museum experience toward something dynamic and interactive. The identity foregrounds sound's multifaceted nature: its historical significance, but also its emotional, cultural, and technological impact.

The system, including logo and full visual identity, was built to feel modern and engaging while welcoming visitors of every age and background into the world of sound.
