Hexcapes

Services

Branding, UI/UX Design

Brief

A modern, recognizable identity for a cybersecurity company built around AI-driven threat prediction for government clients.

Hexcapes is a cybersecurity company building solutions tailored to government needs. Using AI and machine learning, its team works to predict and prevent cyber threats before they happen.

The re-brand set out to craft a recognizable, modern logotype backed by a full identity system — imagery, brand voice, typography, and iconography, plus social templates — all built to meet WCAG accessibility standards.
